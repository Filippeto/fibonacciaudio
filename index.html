FIBONACCI One

Retro-futurist audio for
design-conscious spaces.

The Fibonacci One combines a bold spherical silhouette with a functional acoustic design, creating a compact speaker that feels more like an object than a standard audio component. Inspired by the expressive language of premium audio design, it brings character to computer setups, studios, shelves and creative workspaces.

See it from every angle.

From its spherical black body to the orange pedestal base, rear terminals and sculptural front port, the Orb was designed to look different from conventional desktop speakers.
